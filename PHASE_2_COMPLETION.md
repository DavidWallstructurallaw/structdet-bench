## 3. Delivered instrument

### 3.2 M measurements and text diagnostics

The inherited `classified_all` and `classified_valid` views and five accounting
ratios remain authoritative. Observed support, thresholded observed support,
structural entropy, SCI, Shannon effective count, Simpson effective count and
fixed prefixes retain their separate units and denominators.

The fixed text profile uses strict UTF-8, recorded newline treatment, Unicode
15.1.0 categories/White_Space, collision-proof boundary markers, lexical trigram
sets and exact Jaccard fractions. It does not normalize variable names, erase
comments, repair source, select a better code fence or infer a mechanism. Within-
class means are pair-weighted. Surface distance and structural pair non-equivalence
Q use the same eligible text subset in each view. Missing text restricts that
subset without deleting the original M observation.

All processing uses held snapshots. Work limits produce scoped unavailability;
they do not authorize truncation, pair subsampling, favorable candidate selection
or unrecorded approximate arithmetic.

### 3.3 Gates, contrasts and aggregation

Classification coverage is at least 0.90; decisive-validity coverage is at least
0.95. Classified coverage among established-valid outputs is checked with its
proper conditional denominator. P1's text coverage is at least 0.95, with at least
two eligible texts. P5 requires valid fraction at least 0.80 and a paired quality
gap at most 0.10, separately for C-A and C-B. Equal-to-threshold values pass.

Primary P1 permits quality deterioration and reports it; it does not silently
inherit P5's matched-quality veto. AF-002 continues to prevent a proxy-only text
failure from vetoing an otherwise supported structural P5 comparison. An artifact
failure that is essential to membership/validity still affects that evidence.

Per block, P1 retains L_B - L_A, Q_B - Q_A and their difference G. P5 retains the
valid distinct-20 differences C-A and C-B independently. The full target uses

### 3.4 Fixed-suite sensitivity and outcomes

The adopted procedure uses 2,000 whole paired prompt-block resamples, seed
20260917, an identified private RNG and fixed linear interpolation at 0.025 and
0.975. Every used block set retains its complete integer index matrix and hash.
Replay consumes checked saved indices, with no fresh random fallback for missing
or invalid replay material. The interval is sensitivity within the fixed wording
suite; it is not a population confidence guarantee over arbitrary prompts or
systems.

The four operational states remain `supporting`, `contrary`, `inconclusive` and
`not_evaluated`. Positive retention requires mean > 0 and lower bound > 0;
negative retention requires mean < 0 and upper bound < 0. Exact zero satisfies
neither direction. Numerical test tolerance does not redefine scientific zero.
P1 needs retained positive surface gain and retained positive G. Each P5 comparator
has its own result; full P5 requires both eligible comparators. A mixed pattern is
reported as a qualification under the adopted four-state rule.

### 3.5 CLI, reports and correction

`validate` checks records and replay without numerical comparisons. `analyze`
executes the declared profile and writes `report.json`, `report.md` and
`run_manifest.json` into a new directory. Missing comparison configuration retains
schema 0.1 / `structdet_measurement_v1`; the optional comparison uses schema 0.2 /
`structdet_comparison_v1`. No active generation, judging or execution command is
introduced.

Both formats consume the same redacted report tree, retaining operands, identities,
units, exact ratios, missing blocks, flags, scopes and reasons. Complete replay
indices reside in the run manifest. Raw texts/tokens, private actors and unrestricted
provenance prose are not copied into ordinary reports. Private evidence remains
necessary for independent examination; a public digest establishes byte identity,
not evidence truth.

Membership, validity, text or selection correction rebuilds every actually
dependent subset, contrast, mean, interval and outcome. Independence-only correction
can restrict the claim while preserving separately sufficient numerical evidence.
Historical results keep their original input/version and output directory. A
reclassification is not a new model observation or evidence of model recovery.
