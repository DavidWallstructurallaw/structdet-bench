## 2. Preserve the source's distinctions

**Source-derived: EC §§2.1-2.3, p.3.** Measurement validity includes annotation quality, scoring consistency, prompt control, statistical power, and reproducibility. Distributional validity concerns weights among represented states relative to intended operation. Structural validity concerns which states, boundaries, relations, horizons, and consequences the benchmark represents at all.

**Project rule.** Software arithmetic conformance establishes only the tested part of measurement validity. It cannot establish annotation correctness, structural validity, or deployment representativeness. These three evaluation-level notions also remain separate from an individual output's `validity_status` and from `assigned`/`unresolved`/`unclassified` membership status.

The hero's bounded integer-sorting specification and algorithm-mechanism resolution remain unchanged. Its eight registered mechanisms, six fixed prompt blocks, and finite verification design do not establish a complete taxonomy of algorithms or a representative deployment distribution. Known omissions must be stated at the level of the requested claim. EC §2's omitted deployment mass requires a deployment distribution and a represented subset; this project has not supplied that evidence. Do not estimate it as one minus classification coverage, missing-label rate, or registry coverage.

**Source-derived: EC §3.1, p.5.** EC also considers the structural distribution of evaluation items. **Project rule.** Keep this object separate from SD/SI's structural distribution of generated realizations. Eight algorithm reference classes or 8,995 functional check inputs do not become 8 or 8,995 independently sampled model outputs. A later study of evaluation-item concentration needs its own item population and abstraction. EC's closed-resampling results remain conditional on its stated model and do not add an empirical closure detector to Phase 1.

## 3. Five conjunctive conditions, scoped to the claim

**Source-derived: EC §7, pp.10-11.** An open evaluation architecture requires Structural Validity, External Presence, Source Integrity, Tail Retention, and Corrective Capacity together. Strength in one condition cannot compensate for the absence of another.

**Project rule.** Expand the condition evidence carried through `integrity_assessment_ref` and RF-29 as follows. Preserve EI §6.2's requirement outcomes and claim dispositions.

| Condition | Evidence to record for this project | Inference to withhold without that evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Structural Validity | Intended capability, represented/omitted relations, C/T/resolution/schema pins, mechanism evidence, independent scrutiny, and unresolved challenges to the cut | That the registered schema captures the capability merely because labels agree or calculations pass |
| External Presence | Independently grounded contributions, receipt/use history, selection decisions, and the period for which continuing contact is claimed | Continuing openness from a static archive, a recent timestamp, or an unused submission |
| Source Integrity | Source, knowledge type, transformations, generator/evaluator relationships, exposure, validation basis, and uncertainty | Independence from different provider names, filenames, or deterministic processing |
| Tail Retention | Existing protected-reference designations, review access, rare/high-consequence rationale, and class-specific outcomes where supported | Tail preservation from average performance, or guaranteed occurrence in a frozen generation prefix |
| Corrective Capacity | Challenge, responsible reviewer, scoped action, revision/rollback/re-analysis links, and evidence of resulting action when claimed | Actual correction from a written procedure or an unacted-on alert |

Requirement outcomes remain `satisfied_for_scope`, `unsatisfied`, `unresolved`, or justified `not_applicable`; claim dispositions remain `supported_for_scope`, `restricted`, or `withheld`. Missing evidence cannot be relabeled `not_applicable` for a claim that requires it.

For a claim that an operational evaluation system is open, all five conditions need supporting evidence for that system and period. Do not average their statuses, count four out of five as a pass, or treat a mandatory condition as optional. For a narrow fixture or descriptive report, state its smaller scope and the outstanding evidence. A numeric result can remain `available` while the broader claim is restricted or withheld. No all-five deployment-evidence gate is imposed on arithmetic fixture development.

The four architectural components remain Stable Core, Rotating Structural Frontier, External Incident Stream, and Versioned Tail Registry. Five conditions do not imply five services. UD-006's actual-evidence requirements remain open; UD-007 remains deferred. No automatic model override, withdrawal, or deployment control is added to this offline toolkit.

## 4. Type-appropriate validation

**Source-derived: EC §6, pp.9-10.** Formal claims answer to derivation/proof checks; empirical claims to observation, provenance, and replication; causal claims to discriminating prediction/intervention/counterfactual pressure; coding claims to execution; agentic claims to sustained environmental interaction. Safety and human-consequence claims require their corresponding stress, containment, and real-interaction evidence.

**Project rule.** For the hero, keep these evidence paths distinct:

| Claim | Relevant constraint | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| An imported program meets the declared sorting behavior | Supplied execution evidence for the specified inputs/properties/environment | Finite tests do not prove every admissible case or an algorithm-family identity |
| A program belongs to a registered mechanism class | SC/HERO's control-flow, dependency, invariant, trace, counterfactual, and adjudication evidence as applicable | A sorted answer, function name, self-description, or generic language-model judgment is insufficient alone |
| A metric follows its contract | Independent arithmetic fixtures and software tests | Passing those tests does not validate the imported labels |
| A deployed system is safe, capable over long horizons, or beneficial to users | Appropriate external system/environment/human evidence | The sorting probe provides no such certification |

Execution evidence is supplied from outside the current runtime. Phase 1's prohibition on executing input programs remains unchanged. SD/SI's independent core annotation and sampled Tier 1 audit requirements also remain unchanged. Adding EC does not waive them or claim they have already been met.

## 5. Reopening and anomaly preservation

### 5.1 Two different changes

**Source-derived: EC §§4.4-4.5, pp.7-8.** Distributional reopening introduces reality-bearing cases within an existing represented state space. Structural reopening changes that state space when preserved anomalies expose an inadequate cut. Temporal freshness or repeated item generation alone guarantees neither.

**Project rule.** An evidence-linked correction/intake record may describe a `distributional_reopening_event` or `structural_reopening_event`, or both with separate effects. These are semantic event types for versioned records, not new runtime commands or automatically detected outcomes.

A distributional claim must identify the existing frame, the independent contribution, its reviewed use, and any resulting sample/selection/weight change. Archival receipt alone is an intake event. A structural claim must identify the challenged distinction, old/new schema or task-cut versions, evidence, decision responsibility, and affected results. A version bump, cosmetic rename, unsupported new label, or corrected sample ID alone establishes no structural reopening. A split/merge only qualifies to the extent that it repairs a supported, consequential distinction.

Keep the anomaly, provisional interpretation, reviewer decision, and demonstrated effect separate. Preserve old reports and reassess affected cases symmetrically across favorable and adverse conditions. Reclassifying the same observations does not create new samples. A schema-based change in support/SCI does not by itself show model contraction or recovery. EC's reopening concepts do not supply SI's External Recovery Rate or prove that a model has recovered a missing structure.

### 5.2 Preserve anomalies without manufacturing novelty

**Source-derived: EC §7.1, p.10.** An observation that challenges the taxonomy must remain available in its original form long enough to challenge the cut, rather than being immediately translated into a familiar label.

**Project rule.** Retain the received artifact or access-controlled reference, context, origin, transformation chain, relevant uncertainty, proposed distinction, review responsibility, and disposition. Keep a normalized summary alongside, rather than instead of, the original evidence. This does not authorize public release of confidential content or fetching remote material.

An `unclassified` row can arise from missing bytes, insufficient evidence, a parse problem, out-of-scope material, or a genuine taxonomy challenge. None automatically proves a new mechanism. Keep the reason and any anomaly link while retaining the existing membership states. A challenged mechanism contributes a counted class only through the approved classification/revision process. Conversely, low coverage must not automatically trigger forced assignment or deletion of difficult material.

Existing `evidence_refs`, `correction_ref`, frontier/incident records, RF-31, and RF-33 can carry these distinctions. Exact serialization and method bindings belong to the relevant authorized implementation step. This addendum requires no additional incident service, automatic taxonomy editor, or new anomaly-rate estimator.

## 6. Open-evaluation reporting crosswalk

**Source-derived: EC §10, p.13.** The paper specifies ten minimum disclosures. **Project rule.** Carry each as evidence, an explicit unknown/unavailable state, or a reasoned out-of-scope statement. A blank field cannot imply validation. The following mapping supplements existing groups; it does not change any metric's formula or denominator.

| EC disclosure | Existing report groups | Updated requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Capability claim and intended deployment scope | RF-02, RF-29, RF-34 | State the bounded probe/claim and distinguish any broader requested use |
| 2. Structural cut, represented and omitted conditions | RF-02, RF-03, RF-04, RF-29 | Include known omissions and unresolved cut challenges; do not claim an exhaustive omission inventory |
| 3. Source and transformation lineage | RF-14, RF-29, RF-31 | Preserve knowledge type and transformations as well as origin |
| 4. Contamination controls and known exposure | RF-05, RF-06, RF-14, RF-32 | Separate known exposure, declared controls, unknown history, and tested controls |
| 5. Evaluator identity, relationship, and bias controls | RF-14, RF-30, RF-32 | State actual roles and scoped independence/calibration evidence |
| 6. Class-specific performance and rare/severe states | RF-13, RF-16, RF-17, RF-20, RF-31 | Use approved class/validity accounting and tail designations; do not invent severity or a new weighted score |
| 7. Horizon, recovery, and override where relevant | RF-02, RF-33, RF-34, RF-35 | Declare unavailable/out-of-scope behavior; no agentic test or recovery estimator is added |
| 8. Live/field evidence | RF-14, RF-29, RF-34 | State what actual evidence exists; fixture execution or current source dates do not qualify alone |
| 9. Correction path triggered by failure | RF-29, RF-33, RF-34 | Distinguish planned response, responsible authority, and completed action |
| 10. Date after which a result is no longer current | RF-07, RF-29, RF-34 | Record an evidence-backed review/expiry decision or explicitly state that no such date has been established |

For disclosure 10, proposed semantic contents are `assessed_at`, `current_until`, the responsible reviewer and basis, and reconsideration triggers. These are record contents, not mandatory new top-level schema names. The source supplies no universal 30-day or 90-day validity period. Missing `current_until` remains explicit and prevents an indefinite-current operational claim; a trigger list does not impersonate the date EC requests. Schema, model, evidence, or deployment changes can require earlier reassessment. Expiry of a current-use claim does not erase a historical observation or make its pinned arithmetic unreproducible.

For the current sorting probe, broader live deployment and long-horizon behavior remain unvalidated. Reporting this limitation satisfies disclosure, not the missing substantive evidence. A fixture report must say `fixture_only`, show the implemented calculation scope, and preserve E/D absences. A descriptive imported-data report can remain useful with appropriately restricted claims.
